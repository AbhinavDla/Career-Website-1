Junior Manager

Management
$150K – $300K

Overview

Junior managers work closely with associates as their superior, providing extensive feedback and assistance. They may also serve as substitutes for associates in select cases.

Responsibilities

  • Recruit, hire, and train new members of the sales staff
  • Set goals, analyze data, and develop training programs
  • Direct and organize team operations
  • Monitor associates' work and provide feedback

Qualifications

  • Demonstrated leadership expertise
  • Track record as a top sales or contracting associate
  • Strong communication and organizational skills

Junior Contractor

Contracting
$101K – $177K

Overview

Junior contractors work closely with sales associates to maximize profit on each contractual deal, collaborating to negotiate reasonable terms between buyers and sellers.

Responsibilities

  • Find property owners wishing to sell and put properties under contract
  • Document the entire contracting process
  • Provide contracts to sales associates
  • Collaborate on deal negotiation and profit optimization

Qualifications

  • Ability to identify profitable properties under contract
  • Good judgment for when to hold firm and when to compromise
  • Strong negotiation and documentation skills

Junior Sales Associate

Sales
$112K – $203K

Overview

Junior sales associates work closely with contracting associates to sell contracted properties, managing relationships with outside parties and handling multiple stakeholders at once.

Responsibilities

  • Organize and maintain a list of potential buyers for contractual properties
  • Negotiate deals with buyers independently or under supervision
  • Manage relationships with multiple external parties
  • Collaborate with contracting associates on property sales

Qualifications

  • Strong bargaining abilities and confidence
  • Ability to hold ground in negotiations
  • Good judgment and interpersonal skills
